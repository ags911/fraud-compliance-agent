Wordmark — Satoshi, weight 700, −0.01em tracking. Same geometric family as the mark's linework and the rest of the UI, and the same bold weight used by the product's other headings. Colors — ink #18181b, paper #ffffff, wash #fafafa. Swap ink↔white per mode; everything else about the mark stays identical. Built on a 24×24 grid: a flattened ellipse arc (rx 10.8, ry 3, rotated −25°) drawn as two halves around a 6.4px-radius outlined circle — the back half painted first, then a background-colored disc + the circle's outline on top (so the ring is hidden within the planet's silhouette), then the front half repainted last so it reads as passing over the planet. Stroke bumps up slightly below 32px so it stays legible at favicon size.
